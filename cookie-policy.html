Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

One cookie. Local storage for your picks. That's everything.

The cookie

Dismissing the notice writes swgoh_cookie_notice=dismissed — six months, site-wide, SameSite=Lax. It holds no name, no email, no history. Ignore the notice and nothing gets written.

Local storage, not cookies

Timezone and Territory Battle picks live in local storage. Never sent anywhere — the page just reads them back to render your schedule.

Third parties

No ads, no analytics, no trackers embedded. Google Fonts fetches font files; swgoh.gg links leave the site. Past that border, their policies apply.

Your choices

Wipe site data in your browser and the notice returns, picks reset. Blocking the cookie still leaves a working schedule — the notice just keeps showing up.

Changes

Storage changes land here first, with a new date above.
