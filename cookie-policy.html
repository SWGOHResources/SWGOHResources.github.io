Cookie Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

This policy describes the small amount of browser storage used by SWGOH Resources and how you can clear it.

What cookies are used

SWGOH Resources uses one strictly functional cookie named swgoh_cookie_notice. It stores the value dismissed after you close the cookie notice so it is not shown on every visit.

This cookie is set for six months, applies to the site, and uses the SameSite=Lax setting. It contains no name, email address, account ID, or browsing history.

The cookie is created only after you select Got it in the notice. If you do not dismiss the notice, no consent cookie is written by this site.

Other browser storage

Timezone and Territory Battle choices are saved in local storage rather than cookies. They are used only to remember your display and planning preferences on this device.

Local storage is not sent to SWGOH Resources' server. It is read by the page when it loads so the schedule can use your chosen timezone and TB rotation settings.

Third-party cookies

The site does not embed advertising, analytics, or social tracking tools. Google Fonts may make a request for font files, and links to swgoh.gg are governed by that site when you follow them.

Once you leave this site, the destination may use cookies or other technologies under its own policy. This site cannot read or manage cookies set by another domain.

Your choices

You can delete this cookie and local storage through your browser settings. Deleting them may cause the notice to appear again and reset your saved schedule preferences.

Most browsers also let you block cookies or clear site data automatically. Blocking the functional cookie does not prevent you from using the schedule, but the notice may appear again.

Changes to this policy

If the site's storage or external services change, this policy will be updated and the revision date above will change.
