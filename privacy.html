Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

This policy explains what SWGOH Resources collects, what stays in your browser, and what happens when you follow links to other services.

What this site does

SWGOH Resources is a fan-made schedule and planning tool for Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes. It does not require an account and does not ask you to submit personal information.

You can browse the schedule without signing in, providing an email address, or creating a profile. The site is designed to work as a static website in your browser.

Information stored on your device

The site stores your display timezone and Territory Battle selections in your browser's local storage. These settings stay on your device and are used only to display the schedule you selected.

The site also stores a small functional cookie when you dismiss the cookie notice. This remembers that the notice was acknowledged. It does not identify you or track your activity.

These preferences are optional. You can remove them using your browser's site-data or storage controls, after which the site will return to its default display settings.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google to retrieve those font files. The site also links to swgoh.gg for live event timings; following that link takes you to swgoh.gg and its own policies.

External services may process technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests their content. SWGOH Resources does not control those services and recommends reviewing their privacy policies directly.

Analytics and advertising

This site does not currently use analytics, advertising, behavioral tracking, or sale of personal information.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience schedule tool and is not directed at collecting information from children. Because the site does not require accounts or submissions, it is not designed to collect personal information from visitors.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's features or services change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For a site issue or privacy question, use the Report Issue link on the schedule page to contact the maintainer through Discord.
