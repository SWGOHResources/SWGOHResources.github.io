Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

What this site does

SWGOH Resources is a fan-made schedule and planning tool for Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes. It does not require an account and does not ask you to submit personal information.

Information stored on your device

The site stores your display timezone and Territory Battle selections in your browser's local storage. These settings stay on your device and are used only to display the schedule you selected.

The site also stores a small functional cookie when you dismiss the cookie notice. This remembers that the notice was acknowledged. It does not identify you or track your activity.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google to retrieve those font files. The site also links to swgoh.gg for live event timings; following that link takes you to swgoh.gg and its own policies.

Analytics and advertising

This site does not currently use analytics, advertising, behavioral tracking, or sale of personal information.

Contact

For a site issue or privacy question, use the Report Issue link on the schedule page to contact the maintainer through Discord.
