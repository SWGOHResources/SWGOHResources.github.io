Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

No accounts. No analytics. No ads. This page lists everything the site stores or sends, and why.

Who operates this site

SWGOH Resources is a non-commercial fan project maintained by an individual (Dom) and hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. There is no company, no data protection officer, and no customer database — the contact route for anything on this page is the Report Issue button (top right), which reaches Dom on Discord.

What we collect by default: nothing

Simply reading the schedule sends us nothing identifying. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no email fields, no analytics beacons, no advertising trackers, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing — there is nothing to sell. Like any website, the hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages) necessarily processes technical connection data such as IP addresses to deliver pages; that logging sits with the host under its own policies and is not visible to or used by this project.

What stays in your browser

Your preferences never leave your device unless you switch on the features below. Everything is stored with standard browser storage and is read back only to render the page for you:

Display timezone (swgoh-tz) — how you like times shown. Kept until you change it or clear site data.

Territory Battle picks (swgoh-tb-choice, plus per-side variants) — which TB your guild runs, so phase labels and art follow your selection. Kept until changed or cleared.

Cookie-notice dismissal (swgoh_cookie_notice=dismissed) — a single cookie, six months, SameSite=Lax, holding nothing but the fact you dismissed the notice. The full inventory, including expiry behaviour, is in the Cookie Policy.

Clearing the site's data in your browser settings wipes all of the above at once and the site returns to defaults.

Third parties

GitHub Pages hosts these files and delivers them over HTTPS. Google Fonts serves the typefaces, which means your browser requests font files from Google when pages load. swgoh.gg links leave this site entirely. Discord is only a contact channel if you choose to message. Each of these parties operates under its own policies, which this project does not control.

Legal bases (UK/EU visitors)

Where data protection law requires a basis: local preferences rest on consent via your use of the settings; any residual hosting/technical processing rests on legitimate interests in operating a secure, working website. There is no profiling and no automated decision-making.

How long data is kept

Browser preferences persist until you clear them.

Your rights

You hold the usual data rights — access, correction, deletion, portability, objection and restriction — and because almost everything lives in your own browser, you can exercise most of them instantly: inspect anything in developer tools or wipe it via clear-site-data. Anything else, including questions about these rights, goes through the Report Issue button, and substantive requests are answered within one month.

Children

The schedule is general-audience game information with no accounts and nothing to submit, so nothing is knowingly collected from children.

Security

Traffic is HTTPS-only and stored data is limited to what the features need. No system is impregnable; suspected issues can be reported the same way as schedule errors.

International transfers

Hosting may process data outside your country (including the US, where GitHub and Google operate). Where UK/EU rules apply, the transfer relies on those providers' published safeguards.

Changes to this policy

New features mean new paragraphs here first, with a fresh date above. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated page.

Contact

Corrections, rights requests, or questions about this page: hit Report Issue (top right) to reach Dom on Discord.
