What the service is

A day-by-day planning view over expected Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes event rotations, blended with live game data where available: Grand Arena, Territory War, Territory Battle, Conquest, Marquee and related events, plus countdowns and jump links. No account is needed and none is offered.

Schedule accuracy

Rotation dates are estimates inferred from observed patterns. The game publisher can move, shorten, extend or cancel events at any time, and live data feeds can lag or fail — so any date here can be wrong. Always confirm live timings in-game or at swgoh.gg/events before committing resources, rosters or travel around them.

Display options change presentation only: the timezone picker re-labels identical moments for planning, and saved Territory Battle picks only change which run's labels and art you see. Neither alters the underlying schedule.
